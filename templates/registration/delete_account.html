What is deleted

  • Your username, name, email address and phone number
  • Your password, transaction PIN and login sessions
  • BVN / NIN and any KYC details you provided
  • Your virtual bank account details
  • Your notifications and referral link to other users

What is kept, and for how long

  • Financial transaction records (purchases, wallet funding, amounts, dates and references) are kept for 6 years to meet accounting, tax and anti-fraud regulations. After deletion they are no longer linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Backups that may still contain your data are overwritten within 30 days.

Deleting your account is permanent. After deletion you can register again with a new account, but your previous wallet balance and history cannot be restored.
