Privacy Policy

BT DataPlug · operated by BT Software Services Ltd (“we”, “us”). Last updated: 13 October 2026.

This policy explains what information the BT DataPlug mobile app and website (together, the “Service”) collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating an account you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: username, first and last name, email address, phone number and your password (stored only in hashed form on our servers).
  • Identity (KYC) details: BVN and/or NIN, only if you choose to complete verification, used to create your virtual bank account and meet regulatory requirements.
  • Transaction and wallet data: data, airtime, cable TV and electricity purchases, recipient phone numbers, smart-card and meter numbers, wallet funding and balance, amounts, dates and references.
  • Transaction PIN: stored only in hashed form.
  • Security and technical logs: sign-in events and server logs (such as IP address) used to detect fraud and keep the Service secure.
  • Referral information: your referral code and the account that referred you, if any.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. We do not store your card details; card payments are handled directly by Paystack.

2. Fingerprint / biometric login

If you turn on fingerprint login, the check is done by your phone’s operating system. Your fingerprint never leaves your device and is not sent to or stored by us. To let you sign in without retyping, the app keeps your sign-in details in your phone’s encrypted secure storage. You can turn this off at any time in Profile → Biometric login, which also deletes those saved details from your phone.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account, wallet and virtual bank account
  • To process your purchases and wallet funding and send you receipts and transaction notifications
  • To verify your identity, prevent fraud and comply with the law
  • To provide customer support and improve the Service

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what is needed to deliver the Service, with:

  • Monnify (payments and virtual bank accounts), which receives your name, email and BVN/NIN where applicable
  • Paystack (card payments), which receives your email and payment amount
  • Telecom, cable and electricity service providers and our VTU supplier, which receive the recipient number, smart-card or meter number and plan needed to fulfil your order
  • Authorities or regulators, where we are legally required to

These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

5. Data security

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Passwords and PINs are hashed. Session tokens and saved sign-in details on your phone are kept in encrypted secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password and PIN private.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account is active. Financial transaction records are kept for 6 years to meet accounting, tax and anti-fraud obligations. When you delete your account we remove your personal details (see section 7); transaction records are kept in a form no longer linked to your name, email or phone number.

7. Your rights and deleting your account

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data. To delete your account and associated data, use our deletion page: https://www.btdataplug.com/delete-account/. You can also change your PIN in the app under Profile → Change transaction PIN.

8. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.
